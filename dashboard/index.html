How to read this
Task success
share of runs whose final answer the hidden checker grades right.
Catch rate
of the drafts that were wrong, the share the verifier rejected.
False alarm
of the drafts that were right, the share the verifier rejected anyway.
MCC
Matthews correlation, one score from −1 to 1 for how well rejects line up with wrong drafts (1 = perfect, 0 = chance).
Fixed
wrong draft, right final answer after the revision.
Broken
right draft, wrong final answer after the revision.
Human review
the verifier abstained, its verdict could not be read, or the second check did not accept the revision.
95% CI
bootstrap interval over the 32 tasks; wide intervals mean small differences are not conclusive.
